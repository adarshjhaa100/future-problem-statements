# Domain 5: "Glocal" Integration — Agri-Food, Water, Urbanisation

## Research Summary
- 72% of urban sewage in India untreated (31,841 MLD installed capacity vs 72,000 MLD generated)
- Waste management market: $68.5B (2025), projected $118.9B by 2033 at 7.2% CAGR
- Per-capita freshwater below UN water-stress threshold; agriculture uses 80% of freshwater
- India generates ~62M tons of municipal solid waste annually; only 30% treated scientifically
- 500M+ tons of crop residue burned annually (Punjab, Haryana) — causes Delhi smog, burns $200M+ in nutrients
- Tier-2/3 cities growing at 3-4% annually but infrastructure grows at <1%
- Agritech startups raised ~$5B since 2020 but <5% reached profitability
- 86% of Indian farmers are small/marginal (<2 hectares) — precision ag tech is too expensive for them

## The Axiom List
1. Water, nutrients, and energy cycle in closed loops on Earth — nothing is created, nothing destroyed
2. Agriculture converts solar energy into chemical energy (photosynthesis) at ~1-3% efficiency
3. Pollution is a resource in the wrong place (waste = food for another system — circular economy)
4. Population density drives infrastructure cost per capita — denser = cheaper pipes, but harder logistics
5. Trust is local — rural India operates on community relationships, not contracts
6. Scale matters for economic viability — most agritech requires minimum 5-10 acres to break even
7. Soil health is the foundation of agricultural productivity — degrading at 0.5-1% per year in India
8. Food waste = waste of all inputs (water, energy, land, labor) — 40% of food produced is lost

## The Derivation (First-Principles)

### Axiom 1 + 3 → Linear systems (take-make-waste) violate thermodynamic law; circular is more efficient
### Axiom 6 + 5 → "Precision ag for the 1-acre farmer" is economically unsound — need shared/frugal models
### Axiom 2 + 7 → Photosynthesis is inefficient; soil degradation makes it worse; biochar + regenerative ag improves it
### Axiom 4 + 8 → Urbanization creates concentration of waste (opportunity) and demand (market) — matches nearby

**Logic Chain:**
- A1: Nutrients in sewage → if returned to soil, replace synthetic fertilizer; if dumped, pollute water bodies
- A6: A soil sensor costs ₹5,000; a 1-acre farmer earns ₹30,000/yr → ROI negative without shared infrastructure
- A7: Soil carbon sequestration can offset 5-15% of India's emissions (ICAR data)
- A8: Cold chain gaps cause 30-40% fruit/veg loss; cold storage is energy-intensive
