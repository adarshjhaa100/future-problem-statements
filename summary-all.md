## How to read the columns

- **Impact** — who is affected and for how long. A row with “decades” compounds; a row with “weeks” is acute.
- **Crowd** — SATURATED, CROWDED, THIN or NEAR-ZERO, with named players or the specific gap.
- **Budget** — the cost of the first version, in ₹ and US$ at roughly ₹85/US$1.
- **Team & time** — the minimum viable team and the path to a repeatable deployment, not a demo.
- **Startup ideas** — 3 concepts with a fit tag: team size, months, and the first experiment. All are scoped to 1–3 people and under one year.
- **Work status** — what is already being worked on, the specific open gap, duplicate-risk label (DIRECT/CONTESTED/ADJACENT/LOW-ADJACENT/SPARSE) and confidence (H/M/L).

| Layer | Domains | What they change |
|---|---|---|
