# Domain 6: Creative & Strategic "Taste" Economy

## Research Summary
- India's media & entertainment sector: ~$30B (2024); creative exports $11B+ in 2023-24 (20% YoY growth)
- Gaming: ₹232B revenue, ~500M gamers; one of the world's largest gaming markets
- India has 22 official languages, 30M+ diaspora, world's largest film output
- ~65% of Indians under 35 — demographic dividend for interactive/digital content
- 25% of Indian OTT viewership now from overseas — global audience exists
- Core tension: India is service-outsourcing back-office (animation/VFX) vs creating original IP
- Budget 2026: ₹250 Cr for AVGC content creator labs in 15K schools + new National Institute of Design
- Orange Economy covers industries where value comes from creativity and IP
- Creative MSMEs can't get bank loans — IP not recognized as collateral
- South Korea spends $1B+/yr on cultural exports (K-pop, K-drama, gaming) — India spends fraction

## The Axiom List
1. Scarcity creates value — limited edition, exclusivity, authenticity command premium
2. Attention is the scarcest resource in the digital age (Herbert Simon, 1971) — average human attention span < 8 seconds
3. Cultural identity is a moat — AI cannot replicate authentic cultural experience (yet)
4. Network effects dominate creative platforms — value grows with users (Metcalfe's Law)
5. Taste is non-fungible — curation and judgment cannot be fully automated
6. Distribution determines consumption — the medium shapes the message (McLuhan)
7. IP is a real asset — copyrights, trademarks, and patents have measurable economic value
8. Local language content outperforms English in reach and engagement in Indian markets (KPMG)

## The Derivation (First-Principles)

### Axiom 2 + 3 → Indian cultural content has unique attention-capture potential that AI cannot replicate
### Axiom 5 + 6 → Taste-based curation (human judgment) + digital distribution creates valuable IP
### Axiom 7 + Axiom 8 → Local language IP is undervalued because traditional banking doesn't recognize it as collateral
### Axiom 1 + 4 → Scarcity (limited-edition Indian cultural IP) + network effects = defensible market

**Logic Chain:**
- A2: Attention economy is zero-sum; content competing for attention must be differentiated
- A3: Mahabharata, Ramayana, regional folklore have built-in global audience (30M diaspora + growing international interest)
- A5: AI can generate content but cannot make taste-based curation decisions (what's good vs what resonates)
- A7: K-pop proves cultural IP can be valued at $100B+; India has comparable cultural assets
- A8: KPMG report: Indian language digital consumption growing 50%+ CAGR; English flat
