# Domain 3: Biotech, Health & Longevity

## Research Summary
- India's AI drug discovery ecosystem: ~30 companies, ~$800M total funding (vs China $5B+)
- <5 AI-assisted molecules from Indian programs reached clinical trials (vs China 15+)
- AI can reduce drug development time from 5-6 years to ~1 year (Johnson & Johnson, 2026)
- India's pharma PLI: ₹15,000 Cr ($1.8B) over 6 years — but has NO AI component
- CDSCO has published NO AI-specific drug development guidelines as of 2026
- Chronic disease burden rising: diabetes ~100M, hypertension ~200M, cancer 1.5M new cases/yr
- IISc Longevity India initiative launched; BHARAT study sampling underway
- Per-capita healthcare spend ~$75/yr vs global avg ~$1,100 — cost is the primary constraint

## The Axiom List
1. All biological processes follow physical/chemical laws — thermodynamics, kinetics, molecular recognition
2. Human biology has individual variation — genetics, epigenetics, microbiome differ person-to-person
3. Drug development follows Eroom's Law (inverse of Moore's) — cost doubles every 9 years
4. Early detection of disease dramatically improves outcomes (stage 1 vs stage 4 cancer: 90% vs 10% survival)
5. Placebo effect demonstrates mind-body connection — ~30% of therapeutic response is psychological
6. Information asymmetry in healthcare is extreme — doctor knows vastly more than patient
7. Health outcomes are determined ~20% by clinical care, ~80% by social determinants, behavior, environment
8. Regulatory approval is necessary but slow — FDA/CDSCO timelines create a "valley of death" for innovation

## The Derivation (First-Principles)

### Axiom 3 → Current drug discovery is unsustainable; AI is the only way to bend the cost curve
### Axiom 4 + 7 → Most healthcare spend (70%) is on late-stage disease; prevention is underfunded
### Axiom 6 → Patient empowerment through data and diagnostics can reduce information asymmetry
### Axiom 1 + 2 → Personalised medicine is a thermodynamic/kinetic problem: match molecule to individual

**Logic Chain:**
- A3: It costs $1-2B to bring a drug to market; AI can reduce to <$200M by screening 10B molecules in silico
- A4: Breast cancer caught at stage 1 costs ₹1L to treat; stage 4 costs ₹25L+ → detection is highest-ROI intervention
- A7: ₹1 spent on preventive health saves ₹4-7 on treatment (WHO data)
- A6 + A8: India has 1 doctor per 1,500 patients (WHO recommends 1:1,000) → AI triage can bridge the gap
