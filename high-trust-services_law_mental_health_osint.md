# Domain 7: High-Trust Services — Law, Mental Health, OSINT

## Research Summary
- India has 5 Cr+ pending court cases; 3+ year average resolution time for civil disputes
- 1 judge per 100,000 population (Law Commission recommendation: 50 per 100,000)
- Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) Policy Plan for India by NITI Aayog published 2023; implementation nascent
- 0.75 psychiatrists per 100K population (WHO recommends 1:10,000); 150M+ Indians need mental health support
- Mental Healthcare Act 2017 establishes right to mental healthcare but implementation gap is vast
- Mediation Act 2023 enacted; ODR platforms like SAMA, Presolv 360 emerging but <1% coverage
- OSINT tools exist but no India-specific platform for disinformation, hate speech, or regulatory compliance
- AI use in courts: SUPACE (Supreme Court), SUVAS (translation), but <10 High Courts have AI policies
- India has 22 languages; legal documents available primarily in English — barrier for 90% of population

## The Axiom List
1. Trust is a risk-reduction mechanism — humans outsource verification to trusted intermediaries
2. Justice delayed is justice denied — time value of justice follows discounting logic similar to money
3. Human cognitive capacity is limited (bounded rationality, Kahneman) — decision fatigue degrades judgment
4. Information asymmetry creates power imbalances — the party with more information has leverage
5. Mental health follows the same supply-demand curve as any service — shortage drives price up, access down
6. Law is a system of rules with formal semantics — logic-based AI (not just LLMs) can reason about it
7. Privacy is a precondition for trust — surveillance without consent destroys the therapeutic and legal relationship
8. Reputation is a public good in small communities — gossip and social credit existed long before digital

## The Derivation (First-Principles)

### Axiom 2 + Axiom 6 → Legal AI that reduces case resolution time from 3 years to 3 months = enormous social value
### Axiom 3 + Axiom 5 → Mental health chatbots cannot replace humans but can triage and provide first-line support
### Axiom 1 + Axiom 7 → OSINT for regulatory compliance requires trust (transparency about data sources and methods)
### Axiom 4 + Axiom 6 → Legal document simplification (English → Vernacular) addresses information asymmetry

**Logic Chain:**
- A2: If delay reduces justice value by ~30% per year (empirical), 5-year delay = 85% justice erosion
- A3: A judge handling 200+ cases/month has cognitive bandwidth for <5 min per case
- A5: India has 9,000 psychiatrists for 150M patients = 1:16,667 — impossible to serve
- A6: Legal reasoning is deduction + precedent — AI excels at pattern matching in structured rule systems
