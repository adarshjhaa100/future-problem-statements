# Domain 1: Energy Transition & Climate Resilience

## Research Summary
- India targets 500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030; currently ~50% of installed capacity is renewable
- Transmission expansion trails 40% behind targets (IEEFA/JMK Research FY2025: 8,830 ckm vs 15,253 ckm target)
- 50+ GW of renewable capacity stranded due to inadequate transmission and delayed PPAs
- Grid intermittency requires real-time balancing, flexible generation, and advanced forecasting
- Per-capita freshwater availability below UN water-stress threshold; 72% urban sewage untreated
- Budget 2026 allocated ₹600 Cr for Green Energy Corridor, ₹18,000 Cr for RLDS (smart metering)
- India is 3rd largest energy consumer, heavily import-dependent

## The Axiom List
1. Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only converted (1st Law of Thermodynamics)
2. All energy conversion has inefficiency (2nd Law: entropy increases)
3. Solar irradiance on Earth is ~1 kW/m² at peak — this is the fundamental upper bound
4. Electricity cannot be stored in grid-scale easily — generation and consumption must balance in real-time
5. Economic agents minimize cost subject to constraints — cheaper energy wins
6. Land is finite and has competing uses (agriculture, housing, conservation)
7. Information asymmetry causes market failures — data transparency reduces inefficiency
8. Human behavior follows incentives — subsidies and penalties shape adoption

## The Derivation (First-Principles Logic Chain)

### Axiom 3 + 4 → Problem: Solar is intermittent, grid cannot store excess
### Axiom 6 → Problem: Solar/wind farms compete for land with food and habitat
### Axiom 2 → Problem: Every conversion step (DC→AC, transmission, storage) loses energy
### Axiom 5 + 7 → Problem: Cheap coal still outcompetes renewables when externalities unpriced

**Logic Chain:**
- A1: Sun delivers ~10,000x humanity's energy needs → solar is sufficient in principle
- A2 + A4: Solar's intermittency means either storage or backup fossil capacity → costs rise
- A6: India has ~328M hectares land; solar at 5 acres/MW means 500 GW needs ~2.5M acres → ~3% of agri land
- A5: Current LCOE of solar+storage (~₹4-5/kWh) vs coal (~₹3/kWh) → coal wins without carbon price
- A7 + A8: Without transparent pricing of externalities (health, CO2), renewables adoption is suboptimal
