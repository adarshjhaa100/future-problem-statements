# Domain 4: Defence, Space & Advanced Manufacturing

## Research Summary
- India defence budget FY27: ₹7.84L Cr ($88.7B) — largest ministry allocation, 14.7% of central expenditure
- 2 Defence Industrial Corridors (UP, TN) attracted ₹200B+ investments; 16,000+ MSMEs participating
- iDEX scheme supports 300+ startups on AI, drones, advanced defence technologies
- India plans 52 military satellites by 2030 for space-based surveillance
- Drone PLI 2.0 worth ₹1,500 Cr proposed; over 400 licensed drone startups
- 34,000+ items listed for indigenisation on SRIJAN portal; 10,000 already indigenized
- BEL + RRP Group MoU for defence semiconductors, electro-optics, UAVs (March 2026)
- NetraSemi launching first Indian AI chip (A2000 edge processor) in 2026
- Aerospace & Defence market projected $70B by 2030

## The Axiom List
1. National security is a non-negotiable public good — it cannot be fully outsourced (monopoly on legitimate force)
2. Battlefield advantage depends on information dominance — who sees, decides, and acts first wins
3. Supply chain security for defence items is a national vulnerability — import dependency is a strategic risk
4. The laws of physics constrain weapons: energy density, speed of light, material strength, atmospheric drag
5. Cost of conflict is exponentially higher than cost of deterrence — $1 on deterrence = $10 on war (Clausewitz)
6. Space is a contested domain — orbits, spectrum, and latency are finite strategic resources
7. Dual-use technology (civil+military) accelerates development at lower cost
8. Manufacturing has a learning curve: each doubling of cumulative production reduces cost by ~15-25% (Wright's Law)

## The Derivation (First-Principles)

### Axiom 2 + 6 → Satellites + edge AI provide information dominance; India needs 52 satellites but also ground processing
### Axiom 3 + 8 → Import substitution requires building domestic manufacturing volume to drive down costs
### Axiom 1 + 5 → Deterrence cost << conflict cost; indigenous capability is cheaper in the long run
### Axiom 4 + 7 → Small drones (energy-constrained) need lightweight, efficient propulsion + compute

**Logic Chain:**
- A2: In modern warfare, the side with better ISR (Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance) wins
- A3: India imports 55% of defence equipment → sanctions risk (e.g., Russia-Ukraine affecting spares)
- A8: Domestic production is expensive initially but follows Wright's Law — needs committed procurement
- A4: Drone flight time = (battery energy density × efficiency) / (weight × drag); solving this is physics-limited
